Submit Past Questions & Study Materials

Upload scanned past questions, lecture notes, or PDFs to help fellow ABU Chemical Engineering students. Images are automatically compressed to ~200 KB in your browser without apparent quality loss, and PDFs are optimized before upload.

Tap to select files or drag & drop here JPG, PNG, WebP (auto-compressed to ~200 KB) · PDF (auto-optimized, max 25 MB)

Smart Optimization Built-In

Every upload is processed right in your browser before it touches our Cloudflare R2 storage — saving your mobile data and keeping downloads lightning fast for every student.

  • Images → ~200 KB Automatically High-res phone photos of exam papers (3–8 MB) are smartly scaled and compressed to ~180–200 KB while preserving crisp, readable text.
  • PDF Stream Optimization PDFs are inspected for page count and re-packed with object-stream compression (removing redundant metadata) before upload.
  • Stored Safely in Cloudflare R2 Files are tagged by Level, Course Code, and Category so moderators can publish them straight into the Study Library.
